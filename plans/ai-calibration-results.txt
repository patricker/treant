Calibration notes (Task 2.1 — Snort tile)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Snort's strength ladder is FLAT and NON-TRANSITIVE on 5x5, so the field-score
auto-picker (which assumes playouts => strength) is unreliable here. Confirmed
across higher-n runs:
  - p800 is consistently the top field score (n=20: 58%, n=100: 61%, n=300: 61%)
  - p2000 (temp=0, deterministic) is mediocre/exploitable (40% / 50% / 48%)
  - near-random p8 (temp=3) scores high (~48-51%) because it is hard to exploit
The emitted block flips run-to-run (n=20: easy=p2000/hard=p8 [inverted];
n=100: easy=p2000, medium==hard=p800 [degenerate]). Pasting it would make
"easy" the STRONGEST AI. DECISION: Snort keeps `difficulty: col.difficulty` —
same engine/board as Col, giving a genuine pace-based weak->strong tier spread
(p8 -> p100 -> p2000). Snort is a drawish shallow game; extra search barely
changes measured strength, so tiers are keyed to search budget / pacing.

Col (re-run to verify the ctor change is behavior-neutral for flag=0): ladder is
clean and monotonic (field p8=38% ... p2000=71% at n=20; 37% ... 74% at n=100),
confirming gameplay is UNCHANGED by the added avoid_enemy flag. The auto-picker's
medium pick varies by seed between p100 and p800 (Col's ladder clusters below
p2000, so the midpoint lands near either); Col's shipped medium=p100 remains
valid, so Col's block is left unchanged.
