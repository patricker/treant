# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 2, 2026_

Treant (this website, including the docs, playground, and the Treant Arcade at
`/arcade`, and the Treant Arcade mobile apps) is a free, open-source project.
We keep this simple because we genuinely collect almost nothing.

## What we don't do

- **No ads.** There is no advertising of any kind, and no ad networks.
- **No accounts.** You never sign up, log in, or give us your name or email.
- **No selling data.** We have nothing to sell and wouldn't sell it anyway.
- **No tracking across sites.** We don't use advertising identifiers,
  fingerprinting, or cross-site cookies.

## What stays on your device

Game preferences (chosen theme, sound on/off, language, and similar settings)
are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. They never
leave it. All games — including games against the AI — run entirely on your
device; no game data is sent anywhere.

## Website analytics

The **website** (mcts.dev) uses Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymization to
count page views, so we can see roughly how many people use the site and which
pages they visit. This data is aggregated and not tied to your identity by us.
You can block it with any standard content blocker, and the site works fine if
you do. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for
how Google processes this data.

The **mobile apps** (Android/iOS) contain no analytics at all. Nothing is
collected, nothing is transmitted. The apps work fully offline.

## Children

The arcade is family-friendly by design: no ads, no chat, no external links in
gameplay, no data collection.

## Changes
